Integer bits include sign. Stored states include initial values. Intermediate ranges are profiled at their actual evaluation sites; they are not inferred from final state voltages. Constants are the original encoded 32-bit values. Raw products are measured before fractional rescaling. Diagnostic loops and flags are not proposed neuron datapath values.

Pulse counts and rates use the stated pulse interval; a threshold crossing alone does not establish a complete physiological spike or depolarisation block. Numerical failures are flagged and excluded from recommendations. Headroom is a selected policy, not a proof.
